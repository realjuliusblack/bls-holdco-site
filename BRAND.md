Brand Direction: Option A - Rich Black + Warm Gold

Chosen because BLS operates in a market where the client (physician staffing) is conservative and
credential-conscious. Gold communicates premium and establishes trust without being flashy. Rich
black grounds it as an operator firm, not a startup. The combination reads like a private equity
firm or a top-tier law firm - exactly the register BLS needs for outbound credibility.

Colors
------
Background (primary):    #0a0908  (obsidian-950) - near-black with a warm undertone, not cold
Background (secondary):  #100f0e  (obsidian-900) - card surfaces
Borders / dividers:      #262422  (obsidian-700)
Body text:               #f2efe9  (stone-50)
Secondary text:          #e0dbd3  (stone-200)
Muted text:              #9e9890  (stone-400)
Disabled text:           #6b6560  (stone-600)

Accent (gold):
  Primary CTA:           #d4a843  (gold-400)
  Hover CTA:             #e8c97a  (gold-300)
  Rule / label:          #c49a2e  (gold-500)
  Muted label:           #a07a1a  (gold-600)

Typography
----------
Serif headline:  Fraunces (Google Fonts) - optical, characterful, not prissy. Works at both
                 display and text sizes. Has italic styles suitable for emphasis.

Sans body:       Inter (Google Fonts) - neutral, legible, universal. Does not compete with
                 Fraunces for personality. Loaded at weights 300 400 500 600.

Sizing philosophy: Large numbers at 7xl, section heads at 4xl, body at base/lg.
                   Tight tracking on headings (-0.02em to -0.04em).
                   Wide tracking on labels and eyebrow text (0.18em to 0.2em).

Rules
-----
- No gradient blobs.
- No rounded-3xl card sprawl.
- No emoji in copy.
- No em dashes.
- No LPU cyan (#00CCFF) anywhere.
- Section structure: narrow eyebrow column left, content column right. 3/9 grid split on desktop.
